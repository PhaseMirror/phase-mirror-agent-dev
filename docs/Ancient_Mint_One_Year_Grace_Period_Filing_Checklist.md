### Key Statutory Anchors

- **35 U.S.C. § 102(a)(1)**: Printed publication / otherwise available to the public before effective filing date → prior art against third parties.
- **35 U.S.C. § 102(b)(1)(A)**: Inventor’s own disclosure within 1 year is not prior art against the inventor’s application.
- **35 U.S.C. § 102(b)(1)(B)**: Inventor’s earlier public disclosure neutralizes later third-party disclosures of the same subject matter within the 1-year window.
- **35 U.S.C. § 102(b)(2)**: Analogous exceptions for U.S. patent documents obtained from or previously disclosed by the inventor, or under common ownership.
