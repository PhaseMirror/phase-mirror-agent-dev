\hypertarget{pm-ll-pirtm-001-pirtm-and-lexlean-stratification-not-substitution}{%
\section{PM-LL-PIRTM-001 --- PIRTM and LexLean, stratification not
substitution}\label{pm-ll-pirtm-001-pirtm-and-lexlean-stratification-not-substitution}}

Status: receipt, not a new contract. Date: 2026-10-12

\hypertarget{three-languages-one-joint}{%
\subsection{Three languages, one
joint}\label{three-languages-one-joint}}

LexLean's authoring language is a closed-lexicon CNL. Every word,
symbol, and control is declared by a versioned lexicon package, one
typed IR emits canonical LaTeX and prose-free Lean, and \texttt{SPEC.md}
is the contract. It is not ALP.

PIRTM/MOC is the L0 contractive kernel. The predicate LexLean checked
against the observed compiler is \texttt{‖A·diag(λ)‖₁\ \textless{}\ 1}
in exact rationals. The compiler does not factor a term into
prime-irreducible components, and \texttt{prime=} is a stratum selector
it does not even require to be prime.

\texttt{pirtm-apps} CNL compilation is a third thing. On
\texttt{phase-mirror-agent-dev} it is a closed refusal: the crate is
outside the workspace, and the agent does not become that compiler.

\hypertarget{what-spec.md-already-decided}{%
\subsection{What SPEC.md already
decided}\label{what-spec.md-already-decided}}

§34 is the joint. It models a lexeme's declarations as atoms of a
stratum, reproduces the observed contractivity predicate, and carries
the norm as an exact \texttt{{[}numerator,\ denominator{]}} pair.

§34.3 records a Zeno-Finton signal as
\texttt{κ(index)\ =\ 2\^{}(-index)}. It carries no legal weight. The RFC
3161 timestamp remains the only time anchor, because a timestamp is
meaningful only when it comes from an authority the author does not
control.

§34.4 is the load-bearing sentence: dropping every field this section
defines must leave every §33 check and the §33.6 verdict bit-identical.
An entry that omits the layer is a valid §33 entry. That is
stratification. Substitution would break it first.

The receipt hash is a LexLean hash under
\texttt{lexlean-contractivity-v1}. It is not the compiler's
\texttt{seal\_hash}, and an entry says which of the two it carries.

\hypertarget{what-not-to-explore}{%
\subsection{What not to explore}\label{what-not-to-explore}}

\texttt{0000-PIRTM\ LEXLEAN.md} proposes replacing the SHA-256 identity,
the RFC 6962 inclusion proof, the browser transcription, and the FreeTSA
timestamp with PIRTM primitives. §34 refuses the last of those outright
and does not claim the first three. Prime-indexed identity, a
contractivity receipt in place of an inclusion proof, and an internal
temporal anchor are literature claims the observed compiler does not
compute.

\hypertarget{the-next-witness-if-one-is-wanted}{%
\subsection{The next witness, if one is
wanted}\label{the-next-witness-if-one-is-wanted}}

Hand a LexLean snapshot's declaration graph to PIRTM as atoms of a
stratum. Take back a receipt whose norm is an exact rational. Store it
as a §34 supplementary field. Re-run §33 and require the verdict to be
bit-identical with the field removed.

That is the integration. A merged ALP/CNL compiler, or a PIRTM binary
inside LexLean, is a different project and a forked reading of §34.4.
